# S027 — Cost per delivered and retained bit

`python -m gatewaycx.economics` annualises a declared network and storage investment, varies
utilisation and optical availability, applies RF fallback and delivery efficiency, and calculates
cost per delivered bit and protected retained bit-year.

The result is [`results/S027_economics.json`](../results/S027_economics.json). All monetary, demand,
availability and asset-life inputs are synthetic. This is an executable accounting boundary for
future quotes and demand evidence, not a valuation, customer price or claim of commercial viability.
