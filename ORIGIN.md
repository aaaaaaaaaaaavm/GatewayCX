# Origin and chronology

## What happened

In April 2026, my father and I discussed the growing communications bottleneck between missions,
the Moon and Earth. That conversation connected several things I had already been exposed to at
Avisys Services: terrestrial telecom architecture, 5G, CRM, BSS/OSS, gateways and the less visible
machinery that lets many providers deliver what users experience as one network.

The question I took away was larger than mission communications:

> If people eventually live on the Moon, why should they receive a separate collection of space
> links instead of the Internet they already use on Earth?

I initiated GatewayCX at Avisys Services from that question. Between April and August 2026 I worked
through the relay, optical/RF, traffic-management, delay-tolerant and distributed-compute parts of
the problem. The architecture changed as the actual scope became clearer. A relay-management
platform was not the end product. The end product was a native Earth–Moon Internet, with service
operations as one supporting plane.

## What this record proves

This repository became the public, change-controlled engineering record on 2026-08-27. The April
origin described above is a retrospective account. It is not represented as a backdated Git
history.

Where a future chronology claim depends on an earlier document, email, diagram or calculation, I
will identify that source and its date. Where no contemporaneous source is public, the record will
say so. A plausible history and a demonstrated history are different things.
