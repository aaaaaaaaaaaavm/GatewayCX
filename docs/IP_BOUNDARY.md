plus references to publicly available standards and programmes. It shall not contain confidential
customer information, unpublished vendor specifications, credentials, proprietary Avisys source
code or material copied from another project without compatible permission.

GatewayCX is proprietary intellectual property of Avisys Services. Repository visibility is for
inspection and evaluation; it does not grant implementation, derivative-work, deployment,
distribution, patent or commercial rights. The controlling terms are in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

of earlier versions obtained while it applied. New material and later versions are not offered
under Apache merely because older commits were.

The ownership position recorded by this repository is that GatewayCX belongs to Avisys Services.
That does not authorise a contributor to publish other Avisys material, customer information or
partner data. Each later disclosure and any external commercial or standards commitment still
requires approval from an authorised Avisys Services representative.

Public discussion with a supplier does not create a partnership. A vendor appears in the record
only as a sourced candidate implementation unless a written relationship permits stronger
language.
