# S026 — Lunar data-centre physical trade

`python -m gatewaycx.datacentre_trade` compares surface-shielded, orbital and hybrid compute/storage
classes. It closes a first arithmetic loop across IT and facility power, steady-state radiator area,
radiator and shielding mass, comparative radiation-upset targets and storage energy.

The result is [`results/S026_datacentre_trade.json`](../results/S026_datacentre_trade.json). It is
not a flight design: power generation, batteries, structures, landing, dust, view factors, fluid
loops, spares, maintenance and qualified components remain outside the current inputs.
